Fishtype Support

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Questions, feedback or a problem? Email info@fishtype.app. Please include your Support ID (in Fishtype: Settings > Support ID) so we can help faster.

AI fish identification

  • Fishtype sends your photo to an AI service only to suggest the species. Fishtype does not store your photo. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • If the suggestion is wrong, tap "From list" to pick the fish, or "Type a name".
  • You get 3 free identifications to start. Fishtype Pro includes 20 photos a day with a more accurate AI. Fishtype currently shows no ads.
  • Opening the same photo again on the same device does not use another identification.

Fishtype Pro

  • Restore a purchase: in Fishtype, Settings > Restore purchases.
  • Cancel the yearly plan: iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Fishtype. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • One purchase works on up to 5 devices.

Hide faces, map and translation

  • Hide faces: turn on Settings > Hide faces, and people's faces are hidden automatically (blur or a stamp). Tap a face to show just that person, and drag a mask to move it. Faces are found on your device only. Faces in photos sent for AI identification are always blurred, whatever this setting.
  • Map: turn on the map in Settings, and Fishtype adds a line map and the area name (prefecture, state or country, or the sea off it) from the photo's location. This is worked out on your device only, and the exact location is never saved or sent. For a photo without a location, choose the area in the editor under Info > Map area. You can also change the map size and the language of area names in Settings.
  • If you turn off the location option at the bottom of the photo picker before choosing a photo, Fishtype does not receive that photo's location.
  • Translation (Fishtype Pro): in the editor, under Info > "Method or spot", tap "To English" or "To Japanese" and the AI puts the translation in the field (up to 50 translations a day). Your text is sent only when you tap, and is not stored. You can edit the translation or tap Undo to go back.

Text and photo position

  • On the export screen, drag the photo to frame it, and drag the text to move it anywhere in the frame.
  • The text position is remembered for each template and each ratio (4:5, 1:1, 9:16, 4:3, 16:9).

Saving and sharing

  • If saving fails, allow Fishtype to add photos: iPhone Settings > Fishtype > Photos.
  • To start from the Photos app, open a photo, tap Share, then Fishtype.

Privacy

  • No account, and Fishtype never uses your device's current location. The photo's location is used only when the map is on, and only to work out a rough area on your device.
  • To ask us to delete the data linked to your device, email info@fishtype.app with your Support ID.
